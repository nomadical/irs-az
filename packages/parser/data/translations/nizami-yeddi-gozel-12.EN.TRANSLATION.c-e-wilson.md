When the prince rates an onager as self, and makes a dragon captive to an ant,

Then at the castle of Khavarnaq back, drinking and festive, he disposed the hoards.

Ten camel-loads he sent off on the road, a present to his majesty the king.

Ten did he give to Munzir and his son, with certain other rare and precious things.

The rest he spent with fearless unconcern, free from examiners and auditor.

So such a treasure as he’d brought to light, he dearly got, (and) cheaply did dispose.

(Then) Munzir ordered, Let a painter come and once more paint a picture (as before).
