---
1. Fill in the form: a work, a translation or an author.
2. An editor checks the text against its source.
3. Once approved, it's published on the site. If we have questions, we'll email you.

**What you can send:** public-domain texts, your own translations, or translations you have the translator's permission to share. Give the exact source: book, publisher and year, or a link.
